## Design Context

### Users
Small business owners and founders — non-technical, curious, a little anxious. They run a website (a service business, a SaaS, a blog, a shop) and have heard that AI tools like ChatGPT are sending traffic to websites. They want a straight answer: is this happening to me? They use this tool during business hours on their phone or laptop. They share results in WhatsApp groups, Slack, LinkedIn.

### Brand Personality
Sharp. Grounded. Candid. This tool is the friend who gives you a straight answer, not a consultant who shows you a dashboard. It's not cute. It's not enterprise. It's not startup-y. Think: a well-designed printed report, a newspaper pull-quote, a sharp insight card. The brand voice is confident, plain, useful.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial clarity. White paper, ink contrast. Strong typography — predominantly left-aligned, with the H1 treated as a big printed headline. Not a dashboard, not a SaaS app. The result should feel like a receipt or a verdict, not a chart.

- **Theme**: Light. Used during daytime business hours.
- **Fonts**: Barlow Condensed (hero headings — uppercase, punchy) + Figtree (body — friendly, readable, clean)
- **Colors**: Warm off-white background, white cards, verdant teal-green as primary (the "yes" color), near-black ink foreground
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards, gradient-heavy startup landing pages, dark mode analytics tools
- **References**: A well-designed newspaper front page, a premium printed brief, a single-question survey result card

### Key Constraints
- Everything above the fold on the landing page (mobile-first, 100dvh)
- The verdict/key number on results must be visible without scrolling on most phones
- Must look great as a screenshot shared in Slack/WhatsApp/LinkedIn
- No busy gradients, no glowing effects, no "AI aesthetics"
